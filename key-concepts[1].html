Understanding Cookies

While researching cookies, I learned about different concepts that help explain how websites use information.

These are six important concepts that I think everyone should know.

1. HTTP Cookies

HTTP cookies are small pieces of data that websites can store in a browser. They help websites remember information about a user.

2. Session Cookies

Session cookies are temporary cookies. They usually disappear when the browser session ends.

For example, they can help a website remember that I am logged in while I move between pages.

3. Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies can stay in a browser after it is closed. They have an expiration time.

For example, a website can use them to remember my language preferences when I visit again.

4. First-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are created by the website I am visiting.

They can help websites remember settings and provide a better browsing experience.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party cookies come from a different domain than the website I am visiting.

They may be used for advertising and tracking activity across different websites.

6. Cookie Consent

Cookie consent is when a website asks users for permission to use certain types of cookies.

Some websites show cookie banners that let users accept or reject optional cookies.

What I Learned

Before researching this topic, I did not know there were different types of cookies.

I learned that cookies can make websites easier to use, but they can also create privacy concerns. Understanding these concepts helps me make better decisions online.
