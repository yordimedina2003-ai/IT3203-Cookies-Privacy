Welcome to My Website

We use websites every day to search for information, watch videos, and shop online. However, many people do not know how websites remember their information.

One way websites do this is by using cookies. Cookies help websites remember things like login sessions and user preferences.

In this project, I will explain how cookies work, why websites use them, and how they can affect our privacy.

Why Are Cookies Important?

Cookies are part of our everyday experience on the Internet. They help websites remember settings and make browsing easier.

For example, when I visit an online store, cookies can help the website remember items in my shopping cart.

However, some cookies can also be used to track browsing activity. This is why learning about privacy is important.
